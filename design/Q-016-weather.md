# Q-016: Weather location & use

## Question
Which location(s) should we track? Is there anything to warn about, beyond the at-a-glance view? For example: rain means bring the bikes in, or a hard freeze means cover the plants or drip the taps. These would feed the weather-driven tasks.

## Answer
<!-- answer below this line -->
The location should be configurable. An LLM enhancement would be to produce what would be appropriate warnings based on the location.

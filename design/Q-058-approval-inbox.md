# Q-058: The approval inbox: notifications and expiry

## Question

1. **Notifications:** when should adults hear about new proposals? Immediately, in the daily digest, or not at all (just the badge)?
2. **Expiry:** what happens to proposals nobody handles? An event whose date has passed is useless; a bill may still matter.

**What should the defaults be?**

- C. Digest only, with the same expiry rules as B.
- D. Something else (describe).

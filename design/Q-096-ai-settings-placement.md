# Q-096: Where Settings → AI lives, and whether a new endpoint starts enabled

## Question

## Options
- A. Under 'Sources and rules' after Mail; a new endpoint starts enabled (applied)
- B. Its own 'AI' group; a new endpoint starts disabled until switched on
- C. Placement as A, enabled state configurable

## Proposed answer (UNVERIFIED: applied in M4 by Claude, 2026-11-05; confirm or change below)
A (T-15). The decision thresholds use a 50–95 % slider saved on release with Undo.

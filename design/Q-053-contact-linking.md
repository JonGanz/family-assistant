# Q-053: How do teachers and coaches get linked to a child?

## Question
A teacher's email reaches the right child through an **enrollment** (child ↔ school, grade, class) and **contacts** (teacher ↔ enrollment). Someone has to enter those contacts. Teachers change every year, and new coaches and admins appear all the time.

**How should contacts be created?**

## Options
- A. Manual only: adults add orgs, contacts and enrollments in Settings or the TOML.
- B. Automatic: an unknown sender from a known org's domain who mentions a child's name or grade becomes a contact linked to that child.
- C. **Suggested, configurable, default 'suggest'** (recommended): the system proposes the contact in the approval inbox ('Ms. Lee at Example Elementary → Riley?'), and one tap creates it. Each org can be switched to `auto` (B) or `manual` (A).
- D. Something else (describe).

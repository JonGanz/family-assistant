# Q-042: Which task events send phone notifications (ntfy)

## Question
M2 brings in ntfy (D-010). Each member has a topic and notification preferences. Options for task triggers:

- a task is assigned to you (new or by rotation);
- a reminder before the due time;
- a task becomes overdue;
- a routine nudge ("10 minutes left, 2 steps to go");
- a daily digest (a morning summary of your tasks).

Questions: which triggers should be on by default, should there be **quiet hours**, and do **children** get notifications (on their own phones, or are their notifications sent to an adult)?

## Options
- A. Minimal: assignments and overdue only, for adults only. No quiet hours.
- B. All the triggers above, everyone, with household quiet hours.
- C. **Configurable per member, defaults: assigned + overdue + morning digest on, the rest off; quiet hours 21:00–07:00; children's notifications go to their own topic, optionally copied to a chosen adult** (recommended). Everything is editable in Settings → Notifications. Notifications are batched so a burst of new tasks sends one message.
- D. Something else (describe).

# Q-008: Bill handling depth

## Question
For bills, is it enough to itemise them (payee, amount, due date, then a 'pay' task)? Or do you want history and trends, a budget, or an export (CSV, YNAB, etc.)? Should we parse PDF attachments?

## Answer
<!-- answer below this line -->
Just the itemise + tasks. No need for budgeting and fancy reports.

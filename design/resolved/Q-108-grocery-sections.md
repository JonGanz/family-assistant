# Q-108: Grocery list order: aisles, stores

## Context
D-021 has a separate "Staples check" section. The rest of the list could be grouped by aisle (from the ingredient) to make shopping faster.

## Question
How is the list ordered?

## Options
- A. Grouped by aisle; the aisle list and its order are editable; manual items are matched to the catalogue when possible, else "Other" (suggested)
- B. A flat list in the order items were added
- C. Several stores, each with its own aisle order, and a store picker while shopping
- D. Configurable per device (grouped / flat), default grouped

## Answer
<!-- answer below this line -->
Option A. Though there is also the idea that certain items will often come from different stores, so grouping that way would be nice too. For instance, maybe you get your milk at a standard grocery store, but eggs in bulk from a bulk-style store such as Costco.

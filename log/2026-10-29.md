## 2026-10-30: adding a second adult (user report)
- Grade is only asked for children now (the create form and the edit form of `MembersManager.vue`; an adult's grade is saved as null).
- Diagnosis of the errors: by D-006 (`authz.rs`), `ManageMember` lets an adult act only on children and themself, creating an adult's login needs the same check, and `SetOwnPin` allows only the adult themself. So an adult can add another adult as a member but cannot give them a login or PIN, edit them or reset their password, and the new adult cannot sign in to do it. Not changed: this is a security rule, so it is asked as Q-077 (batch 20) with options (initial login by the adding adult, invite link, household owner, configurable default A).

- Week table not rendering (user report): my own regression. When the table became absolutely positioned (to give other weeks a definite height), the Week stack was still `align-items: start` on the grid (I had dropped the `stretch` in the bottom-spacing change), so it had no content height and collapsed to 0. The full-height grid is `align-items: stretch` again. jsdom has no layout, so no test can catch this; I inferred it from the CSS and could not look at it in a browser, please check.

- Q-077 answered (option A) and implemented, D-088 (server and web by a sub-agent): migration `20261030000001_initial_password`; `Resource::UnclaimedAdult` in `authz.rs` (any adult may edit, create the login of and reset an adult who has no account or still has the initial password; PIN stays self-only); `must_change_password` on login and `/me`, enforced server-side (403 `password_change_required` except `/me`, logout and the own password change); the router guard sends such a session to Account; Members page hides grade and PIN for adults and shows a claimed adult read-only.

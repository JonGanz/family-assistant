## Week view: last day clipped in landscape
- Bug: after the bigger date number (1.9rem) the landscape Week table outgrew its box and the last day was clipped. Cause: `tbody` is a flex item whose automatic minimum height is its content, and its `1fr` rows take their content height from the tallest row (the day header), so the day header set a floor. Fix in `WeekTablePanel.vue`: `tbody { min-height: 0 }` so the rows share the box again, the weekday sits beside the date number (one line less in the header) and the header cell clips instead of growing. The bigger event names (DayCell) were not the cause: cells are absolutely positioned and add no height.
- Run: web typecheck, lint, vitest. Not seen in a browser (no layout in jsdom).
- Open: none.

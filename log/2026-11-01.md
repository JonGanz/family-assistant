## Spacing
- Agenda: gap between days 1.5rem to 2.25rem (+50%). Portrait home: extra 1.5rem under the top band (clock and weather), so 3rem to the row below. Landscape unaffected.
- Week view landscape slot 3.6rem to 3.4rem (text needs about 3.39rem), so two rows fit per cell; confirmed by the user.
- Week view portrait slot is adaptive: up to 4.2rem, shrinking to the text height (3.7rem) so at least 3 rows fit a cell (`portraitSlotRem`, tested). Tried a fixed 4.6rem and 4.4rem first: too big.
- Week view slots adapt in both orientations (`adaptiveSlotRem`): landscape up to 3.4rem, down to 3.1rem for 2 rows; portrait up to 4.2rem, down to 3.35rem for 3 rows. Event box line-height 1.15 and vertical padding 0.1rem to lower the text floor. Cause of the single landscape row: the real viewport is shorter than 1080px, so the fixed 3.4rem slot left room for one row. Not pushed or tagged (v0.1.8 is out without this).

## Whiteboard: pen size popup and shapes
- The three width buttons collapsed into one pen button: tapping it selects the pen and opens a popup of sizes; picking one closes it. New shape tool (icon shows the current shape) with a popup of rectangle, circle, triangle and hexagon; drag corner to corner, outline only, using the current colour and pen size. Tapping without dragging draws nothing.
- Shapes are stored as `{s, p: [corner, corner]}` (old strokes load unchanged); the eraser now measures distance to the line segments, so it works on shapes and on fast freehand strokes. Popups are fixed-position beside the button (the toolbar scrolls and would clip them).
- Web lint, typecheck, test and build passed; not looked at in a browser. Not pushed or tagged.

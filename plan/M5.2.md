# M5.2 Recipe re-import and timer buttons: Implementation Plan

## Why
User instruction of 2026-11-19: re-import one URL recipe at a time (no bulk action) so recipes imported before M5.1 can get sections, keeping their meal plan entries and ratings (D-126); and the kiosk timer card buttons still do not fit their labels and are misaligned after v0.1.34. Checked against the code: `POST /recipes/import` returns a `RecipeDraft`; `PUT /recipes/{id}` (`fa-meals` `update_recipe`) keeps the `recipe` row and replaces sections, lines and steps; `meal_plan_entry`, `meal_template_entry` and `recipe_rating` reference `recipe.id` only, so an update keeps them; nothing references `recipe_ingredient` or `recipe_step` ids. `RecipeEditView.vue` already fills its form from a draft (`fromDraft`). The timer card (`dashboard/timers/TimerCard.vue`) uses hand-rolled `.a` buttons, not the shared `ui/UiButton.vue`.

## Done means
1. An adult sees "Re-import from source" on the recipe page and in the editor of a recipe with a source URL; after a confirmation it imports and shows the result in the editor; Cancel leaves the recipe unchanged; Save keeps the recipe id, its plan entries, template entries and ratings, and line matches of unchanged lines.
2. A failed import shows the error and changes nothing.
3. Every timer card button fits its label at kiosk sizes from 1024×600 to 3840×2160 in both orientations, the buttons in a row share one height and baseline, and they look like the rest of the kiosk's buttons.
4. CLAUDE.md workflow 8: `scripts/ci-local.sh`, `scripts/e2e.sh`, `scripts/compose-smoke.sh` green; CHANGELOG entry; tag.

## Work
1. **API client:** typed functions for every M2 route (over the generated types). **Stores:** `tasks`, `lists`, `definitions`, `routines`, `away`, `notifyPrefs`; household store gains the M2 fields. All react to SSE `tasks.updated`, `routines.updated`, `household.updated`, and `resync`.
2. **Routes and nav:** `/tasks` (tabs Now, Mine, Lists, Suggested for adults), `/tasks/:id` (detail and editor), `/routines`, `/settings/tasks` (sub-tabs Lists, Chores & schedules, Routines, Absences), `/settings/notifications`. Add them to the app navigation (Tasks for everyone; settings for adults; Notifications for everyone, for their own prefs).
3. **Tasks page:** `TaskItem` rows with complete (then a 10-second `UndoToast` calling `/reopen`), claim/release for pool tasks, open detail. The Now tab shows "Mine" then "Household". Suggested shows accept/dismiss (dismiss = skip). An add form ("+ Task") that respects the lists the viewer may add to (children: `child_can_add = own` lists, adding for themself only).
4. **Task detail:** title, notes, due date and time, steps (`StepList`, add and reorder for editors), assignees, priority, calendar display (`Hidden` / `Until done` / `Key date`), and the source (`origin`, a definition link for adults). If-Match on save, with the 409 banner pattern.
5. **Settings → Tasks:**
   - Lists: CRUD with policy, child_can_add, dashboard_add, calendar default, claim time (end of day or N minutes), visibility, colour.
   - Chores & schedules (definitions): schedule as "Every N days after it's done" or "Repeats" (`RruleBuilder`, raw RRULE fallback), start date, due time, lead days, missed behaviour (with a one-line explanation of each, and the D-038/D-049 defaults pre-selected), strategy with ordered candidates (drag or up/down), rotation options, priority, season (from/until `MM-DD`) and condition (validated live through `/task-definitions/validate`, like the weather rule editor, showing the next 5 dates), pause.
   - Routines: members, window, steps with target times, day filter, school calendar picker and keywords, dashboard toggle.
   - Absences: member, from, until, note.
6. **Routines page:** today's runs per member with `ProgressBar` and tickable steps.
7. **Settings → Notifications:** member selector (self, plus children for adults), trigger toggles, minutes, quiet-hours override, copy-to adult, subscribe URL and QR code (the existing `qrcode` dependency), "Send test" with clear success and error states, and a "not configured on this server" notice when `server_configured` is false.
8. **Settings → Household:** Now list size and weights, default quiet hours. **Weather rule editor:** for action `task`, a definition picker and create/suggest mode.
9. **Calendar page:** fetch `/tasks?from&to` for the visible range and render task chips on their due day (checkbox, member colour); `always` chips stay after completion, shown done (D-046).
10. Tests (vitest) for the stores, the definition form mapping (schedule ↔ DTO, defaults per schedule kind), the add-permission filtering, the undo flow, and the calendar merge.

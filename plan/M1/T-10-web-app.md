## Work
1. **API client:** typed functions in `api/client.ts` for every M1 endpoint, using **only types from `src/gen/api.d.ts`**. Mutations send `If-Match` and surface 409 as a typed conflict error. Avatar upload sends the raw body with the file's `Content-Type`. `client.ts` is shared: T-11 imports from it.
2. **Stores** (Pinia): `household`, `calendar` (events by range, keyed by id; `ensureRange(from, to, member?)`), `weather` (latest + advisories), `sources` (sources, calendars, oauth accounts), `members` (extend M0 with avatar and colour). Each exposes `invalidate(kind, data)`.
3. **`useLiveStream` wiring:** one app-level subscription. It maps SSE kinds to `store.invalidate` per Frontend.md §5 (`calendar.updated` refetches only an overlapping range; `resync` refetches everything that is loaded). It uses the T-03 composable if one exists, or reports if the composable is missing.
4. **Routes and views** (Frontend.md §2, §3), with the guards from M0:
   - `/`: **Today** (agenda for today and tomorrow, the weather card, advisories).
   - `/calendar`: agenda, week, and month views, a member filter, busy blocks hatched, and the 'from ↗' link.
   - `/members`: avatar upload (preview, client-side size check of 512 KB, then the server's 413 or 415 shown in plain words) and the colour picker.
   - `/settings/household`: includes the "use this device's location" button (Geolocation API, with a graceful failure), units, the calendar window, and `private_events_as_busy`.
   - `/settings/calendars`:
     - Google connect (hidden when `HouseholdView.google_oauth_enabled` is false);
     - the CalDAV form with "Test & discover", and the ICS paste with "Test" (event count + next 3 events);
     - per-calendar enable, colour, members, and dashboard visibility;
     - source state badges with "Sync now" and "Reconnect";
     - credentials are write-only inputs.
   - `/settings/weather`: the rule list and an editor whose condition field calls `/weather-rules/validate` with a 300 ms debounce, shows the error at its position, and previews `{matches, value, when}`. A chip row inserts fields and windows.
   - `/settings/status`: sources, the weather fetch, backup age, and queue counts.
   - `/oauth/done`: shows the result, then continues to `return_to` (same-origin paths only).

   Add nav entries in AppLayout. Settings entries are adult-only.
5. All dates go through the T-03 tz utilities in the **household tz**. Units go through `useUnits`. No date library.
6. **Tests** (vitest):
   - store invalidation (range overlap, `resync`);
   - the conflict banner on 409;
   - the rule-editor debounce and error display;
   - the router guard for child and adult on the settings routes.

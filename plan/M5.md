# M5 Recipes, meal plan and grocery list: Implementation Plan

## Why
- The user asked on 2026-11-07 to start M5 before M4.1 (D-108). The batch 24 answers settled the shape (D-109 to D-119), and `arch/Meals.md` v1 describes it.
- Requirements: a configurable horizon; generating the list from selected meals with normalisation and merging; adding from the dashboard without a PIN; no export (D-015). One living list, archived after 24 h by default, with a staples check section (D-021). On top: recipe import, images and ratings (D-109); permissions, no cook (D-110); slots with only Dinner shown (D-111); meal templates (D-112); scaling, units, LLM-proposed matches (D-113); a hand-written seed (D-114); stores and aisles (D-115); asking on plan changes (D-116); a Food view in the app and the kiosk (D-117); offline shopping (D-118); food only, no notifications (D-119); several entries per slot (D-120); an aisle order per store (D-121); templates saved from the plan or duplicated (D-122).
- Checked against the code on 2026-11-08 (survey of the conventions; file references in the briefs):
  - repos are unit structs taking `&mut PgConnection` (`TaskListRepo`), and versions are checked with `UPDATE … WHERE version = $n` plus `conflict_or_missing`;
  - `db_enum!`, `id_type!` and the per-module `dto!` macro exist in `fa-domain`;
  - RLS uses the M4 `FOREACH … tenant` block;
  - jobs use `JobHandler` plus each crate's `handlers()` / `default_schedules()`, wired in `bins/fa-server/src/wiring.rs`;
  - live updates go through `ChangeBus::publish(conn, household, kind, &json!({}))`;
  - `DashboardView` (`fa-domain/src/api.rs`) is OpenAPI-only and is built in `fa-api/src/feeds.rs::get_dashboard`;
  - `Action::ALL` has 32 entries; `AddGroceryItem` is allowed for everyone and unused;
  - the per-household defaults hook is `fa_weather::ensure_default_rules`, called from `setup::setup_household` and `wiring::seed_default_rules`;
  - the M4 recipe route fetches with private hosts hard-coded off, while calendars honour the `allow_private_hosts` runtime setting;
  - `meal_plan_horizon_weeks` exists (default 2) but no route edits it;
  - the kiosk bar is Home, Week, Whiteboard (`dashboard/DashboardCanvas.vue`);
  - the e2e stub server (`e2e/stubs/server.mjs`, port 18091) serves no HTML yet.
- New dependencies: `pluralizer` 0.6.0 and `image` 0.25.10 (MIT or Apache-2.0, checked with `cargo search` and `cargo info`). `pg_trgm` is in the Postgres image (checked). Names grepped against the migrations: no `recipe`, `ingredient`, `aisle`, `grocery_store`, `meal_*`, `grocery_*` or `meals_*` table exists.

## Done means
1. **Import:** an adult imports a recipe from a URL (a JSON-LD or microdata page, else the LLM), from pasted text (LLM), or types it. Every path opens the draft in the editor, and nothing is stored until Save. The image is stored as a resized copy; an adult can upload one.
2. **Normalising:** on save each ingredient line is parsed, cleaned, singularised and matched (canonical name, then alias, then trigram). Unmatched lines get an LLM proposal when an endpoint is configured. An adult accepts it or picks another match, and the pick becomes an alias.
3. **Library:** search by title and ingredient, tag chips, favourites, last planned, a household note, and each member's own 1 to 5 rating.
4. **Plan:** the meal plan shows week rows from the previous week to the horizon, today inverted, and the visible slots (only Dinner for a new household). Adults add, edit, move, swap and delete entries; a slot holds several entries (D-120).
5. **Templates:** an adult builds an N-day template in the editor, saves one from a plan range, or duplicates one, and applies it from a start date. A per-slot Replace/Add list comes first, with Add preselected for slots holding free text (D-120, D-122). The result is one change with Undo, and editing either side leaves the other alone.
6. **Generating:** "Make grocery list" from a range or picked meals scales, converts within a dimension, merges, and puts staples in the Staples check. Running it twice adds nothing, and it can be undone.
7. **Plan changes:** a plan change that touches unchecked generated items shows the impact and asks whether to update the list.
8. **The list:** grouped by store, then that store's aisle order (hidden aisles go to Other, D-121). Anyone adds and checks items, the kiosk without a PIN; only adults edit and delete. Checked items archive after the configured hours. Every client updates live.
9. **Offline:** an open shopping list keeps working offline (queued adds and check-offs replay idempotently). Over HTTPS the Food view also opens offline.
10. **Kiosk:** a Food view (Meal plan, Shopping list) and a `meals` panel (Family hub preset). The Week view shows each day's meals. The app's Today shows today's meals.
11. **Settings → Meals:** slots, the horizon, stores with their own aisle order, aisles, the catalogue with staples, aliases and merge, and the `meals_settings` toggles.
12. **New households** get the seed (about 300 ingredients, aisles, slots, the list, settings), and existing households get it at the next startup.
13. Closing checklist of CLAUDE.md workflow 8:
    - `scripts/ci-local.sh`, `scripts/e2e.sh` and `scripts/compose-smoke.sh` are green;
    - 0 dangling volumes;
    - the README (Food usage, roadmap) is updated and its steps are verified by running them.
